# Community Questions
Each published question is a discussion topic visible across schools to signed-in
members. Publication is immediate, explicitly selected by the user on 2026-09-07.
All recognized roles may publish and comment, including Viewer; this community
capability does not grant Viewer exam or personal-bank write permissions.

Members create a question or explicitly share a question they own in their bank.
Nothing private is automatically published. Shared topics contain independent
question snapshots without private provenance metadata. Authors may withdraw their
topics/comments; only Super Admin can moderate other members across the system.
Deleted comments retain a placeholder to preserve reply context. Deleted topics
are unavailable for new reads, comments or copies. Existing personal copies survive.

Lists support search, grade/type/difficulty filters and pagination. Topic pages
show answer details on demand, threaded reply references and discussion forms.
Teachers and administrators may save a public question into their own bank.

Public snapshots include only supported teaching-content fields at every nested
level. Private answer citations, source metadata and internal exam/question ids
are excluded on publication, reads and copies, including historical topics.
Answer details expose retained choice/statement explanations, key points and
essay grading guides. Private source bank records remain unchanged.

## Rich Snapshot Compatibility

Published question snapshots may carry the validated optional rich-content
blocks. Images are embedded PNG/JPEG and diagrams are declarative objects; no
raw HTML or remote export fetch. Public snapshots still exclude private source
references, teacher revision metadata and original generation metadata.
Legacy text-only community questions remain supported.
